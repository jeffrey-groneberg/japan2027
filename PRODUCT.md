# Japan & Vietnam 2027

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, explicitly selected by the user. The published
site must not require a framework, a server, an account or a build step.

## Product purpose

Turn the supplied German travel itinerary into a usable interactive travel
planner, published on GitHub Pages.

## Users

The people planning and taking the trip described in the supplied itinerary.

## Capabilities and constraints

- Preserve the source itinerary's dates, destinations, daily activities,
  transport instructions, accommodation alternatives, booking links, budget
  estimates and booking deadlines.
- Later user-directed simplification removes all budget UI, references to the
  input template, the historical-source tab, the print feature and the entire
  footer from the user-facing site. This overrides the initial presentation
  scope; factual source data may remain internally for provenance.
- The source includes Vietnam as well as Japan; retain both countries.
- Keep the itinerary in German.
- Distinguish source estimates and suggestions from confirmed bookings or
  independently verified travel information.
- Personal selections and checklist progress belong to the current browser;
  there is no shared account or remote storage.
- GitHub Pages is a public publishing target, not a private travel vault.
- The user requested concrete daily sequences, official information links,
  plain-language explanations of JR, rail operators and where to buy tickets.
- Show individual sightseeing and shopping locations on OpenStreetMap, with
  matching coordinate-based Google Maps links.
- Keep descriptions, official links, navigation and culinary advice directly
  at each route point. A map-pin icon opens the complete shared day map in a
  modal, not a separate places/map tab; its numbers must match the actual lane
  points and its close action must remain unmistakable.
- Include both grocery / everyday shopping and souvenir / leisure shopping
  around the planned bases. Do not invent the private Tokyo apartment address.
- Check every external link individually and disclose access restrictions
  rather than presenting a blocked booking page as verified.
- The user requested visually distinct daily points with less scrolling.
  Keep an immediately scannable day overview, with full detail, maps and
  transport in clearly labeled on-demand areas rather than one long column.
- Map tiles may contact OpenStreetMap only after an explicit map-loading action.
- Show only materially different plans as complete parallel route lanes on a
  shared approximate time axis. Put concrete transport from the preceding stop,
  pauses and deliberate free time in the lane. Pacing-only choices become flex
  notes instead of visually duplicate lanes.
- The user explicitly chose a culinary recommendation for every individual
  sightseeing place or district, not only one per city. Explain what makes it
  worthwhile, what may suit children, practical access restrictions and links.
- Previously fetch-blocked published links must be checked in a visible
  browser canvas and its loaded DOM, not declared unavailable from fetch alone.
- User-directed rescheduling puts Shibuya/Harajuku on 5 July and Ueno Zoo first
  on 7 July, avoiding the regular Monday closure. The later explicit user
  choice moves DisneySea to Friday 9 July and teamLab/Odaiba to 6 July;
  do not propose Miraikan on its normal Tuesday closure. Retain existing-ticket
  caveats rather than implying reservations move with the plan.
- Add only Ghibli from the requested anime ideas: a reserved-ticket alternative
  on 8 July, not a second excursion on top of Mount Takao. The official
  Pokémon Center SHIBUYA is also explicitly requested, alongside Nintendo
  TOKYO in PARCO on 5 July.
- The user chose real location photos plus Japanese illustrations for the
  POI highlights. Keep the collapsed overview compact, license/credit real
  imagery and label illustrations rather than passing them off as photographs.
- Provide a separate family-oriented Kulinarik section focused only on Tokyo,
  Kyoto and Osaka: interactive meals and views without adult-only bars or
  formal fine-dining obligations. Separate verified walk-ins, recommended
  reservations and mandatory advance bookings.
- Research and integrate fitting fireworks, parades and festivals. Date-specific
  2027 announcements must be distinguished from traditional annual rules.
  Keep festival options genuinely optional, give family crowd/exit guidance
  and do not silently change the stays or fixed park day to chase an event.
- UZUMASA Kyoto Village is explicitly selected as a Ninja alternative replacing
  the 13 July Nara day. Include the hidden-mechanism house and physical ninja
  maze with separate ticket/safety/transit facts; never stack both day trips.
- Include HIRAKUZA as family Sumo entertainment in both cities: formal Kaiseki
  in Ginza as a 10 July route replacing Akihabara, and the casual Bento version
  in Osaka on 18 July. Identify both as English-language entertainment rather
  than professional Grand Sumo, preserve each child-meal/booking rule and tell
  the family to choose one location rather than scheduling both.
- In Vietnam, retain only Hoi An, Marble Mountains and Ba Na Hills as external
  excursions. All other resort days are intentionally unstructured hotel,
  pool and beach days rather than optional replacement trips.
- Availability findings apply to the exact offered dates and room categories.
  An expired flight offer does not establish that a route does not exist;
  distinguish an official route announcement from a confirmed dated booking.

## Brand commitments

original Japanese rhythm-arcade visual language, without copying game
characters, logos, artwork, music or other proprietary assets.
The user clarified: Japanese-based, playful, but clean. Original SVG artwork
is an explicitly permitted asset medium.
